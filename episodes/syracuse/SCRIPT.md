## Characters
- **Archimedes**: the calm genius who never looks up from his circles while his machines wreck the Roman fleet.
- **Marcellus**: the proud Roman general; his helmet plume wilts after every failure and springs back with every new hope.
- **Lucius** (fictional legionary): nervous and unlucky. He dangles from the lifted ship, becomes a pincushion, flees from a stick, counts the stones, and gets the last scare.

## The visual story
A huge Roman fleet sails at Syracuse. A giant claw lifts a warship onto its tail while Archimedes draws circles. Boulders, arrows and a crushed ladder-ship
escalate the misery until Marcellus pours the sea out of his helmet. The army learns to flee from sticks, waits, and finally gets in
by counting stones: "Rome beat the mathematician… by doing math." Then a stick pokes over the wall, one last time.
