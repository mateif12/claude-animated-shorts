## Facts used (see RESEARCH.md for status of each)
- 539 BC: Babylon taken by Cyrus II's forces. Nabonidus Chronicle (BM 35382): battle at Opis, Sippar taken, Babylon
  entered "without battle"; Cyrus entered 17 days later.
- Two main walls + moat (Imgur-Enlil / Nēmet-Enlil); glazed-brick gate with dragons and bulls (Ishtar Gate).
- Herodotus 1.179 (chariot on the walls — framed as his claim) and 1.191 (river diverted into a marsh, water to
  mid-thigh, entry along the riverbed during a festival). Xenophon, *Cyropaedia* 7.5: a similar river-entry story.
- Correction to the brief: Herodotus wrote about a century after 539 BC (not "centuries later").
