## Creative and accuracy decisions
- **Characters:** original Roman cast in the skill's style (bean heads, plum ink, crescent shading, blinks). Labourers wear belted earth-tone tunics; the engineer wears an off-white tunic and red sash; citizens wear coloured tunics/stolae.
- **Open channel:** real aqueduct channels were usually covered with slabs. The film leaves the channel open so the water can be seen. This is a deliberate readability choice, noted in `research/RESEARCH.md`.
- **Water speed** in shot 10 is compressed for drama; the narration states the real figure ("about a day").
- **No on-screen text** (skill rule). A separate subtitle file is supplied: `final/subtitles.srt`.
